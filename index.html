There when you need it

In your menu bar.
Out of your way.

Close the window and keep going. The moon icon stays within reach, with no extra icon in your Dock.

  1. Choose your image. Make Focus feel different.
  2. Click Start. Turn on a Focus mode in macOS.
  3. Return to your desktop. Your original wallpaper reappears when Focus ends.

A small setup, once.

Open the download and drag Focus Wallpaper into Applications. Choose an image and click Start. No Shortcut or LaunchAgent is needed.

If macOS asks for file access

Pause monitoring, then choose Allow Focus File Access… from the moon menu. Select ~/Library/DoNotDisturb/DB. Access is remembered when you reopen the app.

Some macOS versions may require broader Full Disk Access. See the setup instructions before granting it.

Good to know.

One image is used for all Focus modes. It appears as a temporary desktop layer, leaving your original wallpaper and lock screen untouched.

Compatibility & download details

The download is for Apple silicon. The project targets macOS 12+, but it reads an undocumented Focus state format, so macOS updates can affect detection. The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized.

Monitoring starts when you click Start. Pause or Quit removes the Focus image. Read the current limitations or build from source.
